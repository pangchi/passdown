What this is

A shared log for handing off information between shifts. Log an issue, a task, or anything the next shift should know — everyone on the team sees it, filters it, and can follow up on it. No login required; just pick your name and go.

Adding an entry

Fill in the "New entry" form on the left. Here's what each field means:

  • Name(s) — who this entry is from. Type or pick a name, then click Add (or just press Enter) — you can add more than one person to the same entry. Don't see your name? Click the + next to "Name(s)" to add yourself to the team list.
  • Shift — Day or Night. Auto-picked based on the time of day, but change it if you're logging something for the other shift.
  • Tool / Entity — which tool or piece of equipment this is about. Start typing to search.
  • Chamber (optional) — free text, if it applies.
  • Category — PM, CM, SPC, or whatever your site uses. Click the + next to "Category" to add a new one if it's missing.
  • Task start / Task end (optional) — when the task happened. Task start is pre-filled with the current time; click into Task end and it'll default to match Task start, so you just adjust the time forward.
  • Problem Statement — required. What's going on.
  • Action Item(s), Current Status, Follow up (all optional) — fill in whichever apply. Leave the rest blank.
  • Attachment(s) (optional) — drag and drop files onto the box, or click it to browse. Add files in more than one round if you need to — they pile up rather than replacing each other. Click the "×" next to a file to remove it before submitting.

@mentioning someone or #tagging something

In Problem Statement, Action Item(s), Current Status, or Follow up, type @ to bring up a list of team members — pick one to mention them. Type # to bring up tags that have been used before — pick one, or just keep typing to make up a new tag on the spot (like #urgent or #chamber-a). Both show up as clickable bubbles on the entry once it's saved — more on that below.

Reading the log

Entries are grouped by day, newest first. Each one shows who it's from, the shift, tool, category, and any hashtags or mentions — as colored bubbles. Click any bubble to filter the log to just that name, shift, tool, category, tag, or location — click it again (or click "All") to clear that filter. You can combine as many filters as you like at once.

The filter bar above the log has the same controls, plus a search box that looks for a word or phrase anywhere in the entry's text (Problem Statement, Action Items, Current Status, Follow up). Use the 🔍 button or just press Enter to search, and ✕ to clear the search without losing your other filters.

Working with an entry

Each entry has three buttons at the bottom:

  • Copy — copies the entry's text to your clipboard, ready to paste into an email, chat, or wherever.
  • Edit — change the note, task start/end, or who it's from. Nothing is ever silently overwritten: every edit keeps the previous version, viewable on the edit page.
  • Delete — removes it from the log. It isn't actually erased from the system, just hidden — ask an admin if something needs to be permanently recovered.

Team names and categories

Use the Manage names and Manage categories links at the top of the page (or the "+" shortcuts next to those fields on the New Entry form) to add or remove people and categories. Anyone can do this — no special access needed.
